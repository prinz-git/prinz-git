About

Quality isn't a phase. It's an engineering discipline.

My career has moved from QA Engineer to Senior QA / Test Consultant to QA Lead, and now into full-stack QA, TestOps, and AI-assisted quality engineering — across healthcare, aviation, eCommerce, and SaaS products.

I enjoy the parts of the job most people skip: untangling ambiguous requirements, debugging failures that don't reproduce cleanly, and designing automation that actually survives contact with a real release schedule. Lately that includes bringing AI tools into the workflow — not to replace judgment, but to move faster with it.

How I Work

Six principles I don't compromise on.

01

Understand the Risk

I don't test everything equally. I identify what can hurt users and the business most.

02

Shift Left

Quality starts before execution — with requirements, architecture, and acceptance criteria.

03

Automate Intelligently

Automation should provide fast, reliable feedback — not create another maintenance problem.

04

Test Beyond the UI

APIs, databases, integrations, contracts, and infrastructure matter.

05

Investigate Failures

A failed test is a signal. I investigate the system, not just the assertion.

06

Use AI Responsibly

AI accelerates quality engineering while human judgment remains accountable.
